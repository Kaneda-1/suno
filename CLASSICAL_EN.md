# MASTER PROMPT FOR CLASSICAL + FUSION SONGS

Use this structured prompt to create highly detailed classical fusion songs in Suno Pro.

---

## 🏗️ Master Prompt Template

"Create a song for Suno Pro with:

### BASE CLASSICAL MUSIC:
- **Composer(s):** [e.g., Bach, Mozart, Beethoven, Vivaldi, Chopin]
- **Specific Works:** [Name the exact works with catalog numbers: RV, BWV, K., Op.]
- **Specific Movements:** [Which movements or sections to use: Allegro, Andante, etc.]
- **Specific Passages:** [Exact measures or themes if known]

### MUSICAL FUSION:
- **Additional Style(s):** [e.g., flamenco, jazz, rock, pop, electronic]
- **Specific Instruments:** [e.g., Spanish guitar, heavy drums, analog synths]
- **Particular Techniques:** [e.g., rasgueado, tremolo, virtuosic solos, gated reverb]

### VOICE AND LYRICS:
- **Voice Type:** [e.g., Operatic soprano, gravelly baritone, ethereal whisper]
- **Language:** [English, Spanish, etc.]
- **Lyrical Theme:** [e.g., unrequited love, epic battle, cosmic nostalgia]
- **Emotional Focus:** [e.g., vulnerable, powerful, melancholic, triumphant]

### SPECIFIC STRUCTURE:
- **Duration:** [Short ~3min / Medium 4-5min / Long 6min+]
- **Musical Correspondences:** [Which classical theme matches which lyric section]
- **Emotional Progression:** [How the energy evolves throughout the song]

### TECHNICAL DETAILS:
- **Specific Keys:** [e.g., D Minor, G Major]
- **Approximate Tempos:** [BPM for different sections]
- **Special Effects:** [e.g., Cathedral reverb, spatial depth, binaural recording]"

## 🌟 Examples of Perfect Prompts

### Example 1 - Classical-Rock Fusion
"Create a song using Beethoven's 9th Symphony (Ode to Joy) fused with heavy metal electric guitar and powerful drums. Epic male voice singing in English about overcoming adversity. Let the 'Ode to Joy' melody be the main chorus played with high-gain distorted guitars."

### Example 2 - Baroque-Jazz Fusion
"Use Pachelbel's Canon in D Major fused with swing jazz. Add tenor saxophone, jazz piano, and a walking upright bass. Sultry female voice singing in English about lost love. Use the Canon's progression as the harmonic base for jazz improvisations."

### Example 3 - Multi-Composer Fusion
"Combine Beethoven's Moonlight Sonata (1st mov), Für Elise, and Mozart's Rondo alla Turca with ambient electronic textures and synthesizers. Ethereal female voice in English about dreams and nostalgia. Use Moonlight Sonata for intimate verses and Rondo alla Turca for energetic choruses."

## 🎯 Key Elements to Include

### ✅ Mandatory:
- **Specific works** with catalog numbers (RV, BWV, K., Op.)
- **Exact movements** (1st movement, Allegro, Andante, etc.)
- **Voice type** and desired range
- **Clear lyrical theme**
- **Specific fusion styles**

### ✅ Highly Useful:
- **Specific measures** if known
- **Preferred musical keys**
- **Concrete additional instruments**
- **Desired emotional progression**
- **Approximate duration**

### ✅ Optional but Powerful:
- **Specific instrumental techniques**
- **Desired production effects**
- **Artistic references** ("sounds like X artist")
- **Intended context** (e.g., for a film score, wedding, etc.)

---

## ✨ The Magic Formula:
`"Specific Classical Work + Fusion Style + Defined Voice + Emotional Theme + Musical Correspondences"`

With this structure, you can create incredibly detailed songs where every musical section has a specific purpose and exact emotional correspondence.
